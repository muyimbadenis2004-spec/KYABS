About Our School

Welcome to Kyabazinga College Kamuli

Kyabazinga College Kamuli is committed to providing quality education while nurturing discipline, leadership, talent, creativity and cultural values.

Our goal is to create an environment where every learner can discover their abilities, develop confidence and prepare for higher education and responsible citizenship.
